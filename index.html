Our Story & Roots

A Pillar of Compassion in Soweto

Losing someone you love is an agonizing experience. At Zimeleni Funeral Services, our name reflects our dedication: to support and uphold families when they need it most.

Operating from our head office at 415 Dimakatso / Limakatso Street in Central Western Jabavu, and reaching across Orange Farm and surrounding townships, we understand our people's customs. We understand the significance of a dignified night vigil (umlindelo), family comfort, and giving our ancestors the reverence they deserve.

Whether you need immediate 2am home collection, assistance with local hospital mortuary releases, or guidance in choosing an enduring granite headstone, you will always be greeted with patience, dignity, and cultural honor.
